- Live demo: https://arya123-sudo.github.io/crunchy-cafe-demo/

- Rating shown (Google 4.7, 594 reviews) is from the cafe's public Google listing, researched 30 Oct 2026; the cafe previously had no website of its own, only a Zomato share link.
- Photos are royalty-free stock images, credited in the page footer.
- This is a demo website made for CRUNCHY CAFE — it is not the cafe's official site, and the cafe has not been contacted yet.
